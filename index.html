How to read this map

Layers draw in the order listed, from top to bottom. Each starts at 60% opacity. Blended colors are not a combined measurement; compare percentages in the watershed readout.

Legends show full opacity. Missing values are transparent. Rivers, lakes, and impairment lines stay above agriculture. Crop percentages describe cropland; treatment percentages describe farmland area, not chemical amounts.
